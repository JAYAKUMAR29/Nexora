OUR INNOVATION

Our team is developing an AI-powered intelligent assistant designed to help users access and understand information related to Indian Standards and BIS services. The system uses modern AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation techniques to retrieve relevant information from official BIS documents and provide clear, reliable responses.

AI POWERED

Uses generative AI to understand user questions and provide intelligent responses.

RAG TECHNOLOGY

Retrieves relevant information from the BIS knowledge base before generating answers.

SMART SEARCH

Uses semantic search and vector embeddings to find relevant BIS information.

ACCESSIBLE INFORMATION

Helps users find important BIS information faster and more efficiently.

PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

Our architecture combines document retrieval, vector search, generative AI, backend services, and a user-friendly interface to create a reliable BIS information assistant.
